ARCHITECTURE

Boundary solver  prototype/src/boundary.rs
  BoundaryStrategy trait + BoundarySolver
  ExactMiter            exact common boundary on both original offsets
  HybridAtJunction      exact where it exists; junction tangent extension on
                        the missing side; one shared seam on both extended
                        side surfaces
  HybridAtLimitTangent  AOIM negative control; typed UncoveredProfile refusal
  common input: PathFrame/BoundaryInput (position, tangent, normal, curvature,
                derivatives, valid domain, placed profile, original paths,
                extension, K, samples per segment)
  common output: JointBoundary (seam curves, seam vertices, strategy,
                diagnostics: exact/junction samples, first junction alpha,
                alpha range, max residual)

Surface builder  prototype/src/level_b_geometry.rs
  NURBS side surfaces for the extended pieces

B-rep builder    prototype/src/direct_brep.rs
  build_direct_joint: shared vertices/edges/faces, caps, shell
  no Boolean / ProjectAndTrim / StitchFaces

CLASSIFICATION

  Piece::is_linear samples the original curvature. A seam on a straight piece's
  extension is still an exact offset intersection; a seam on a curved piece's
  extension is the junction tangent continuation.
  ExactMiter refuses with UncoveredProfile if any sample is in the second
  category. HybridAtJunction accepts both. HybridAtLimitTangent refuses when a
  junction is needed.
