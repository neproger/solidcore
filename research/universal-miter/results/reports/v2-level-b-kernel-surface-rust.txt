ARCHITECTURE

KernelCurve<'a>
- Adapts solidcore::curve::curve::Curve to the Level A PlanarCurve trait.
- Natural parameter is preserved; all evaluations go through
  Curve::evaluate(k, u, 2, true, false), so rational weights are kept.
- The 3D point and derivatives are projected onto the local path-plane axes
  (origin, x_axis, y_axis). normal = rot90(tangent);
  curvature = cross(first, second) / speed^3; speed <= 1e-15 is ZeroSpeed.

AnalyticSweep
- S(v, u) = P(v) + alpha(u) * n(v) + beta(u) * plane_normal,
  n(v) = plane_normal x tangent(v).
- section(v) is the input profile moved by
  Axis2Placement::from_to(start_frame, current_frame).
- The profile must lie in the start normal plane (normal, plane_normal). A
  profile with a component along the start tangent is outside this construction.

build_swept_surface
- Adaptive cross sections; BSplineSurface::create_skinned_surface with
  cross_section_surf_param = U and the requested loft degree.
- Two-sided geometric deviation: analytic -> NURBS via Surface::drop_point
  (NoObject), NURBS -> analytic via an independent sampled/golden
  closest-point search. This checks points between the fit sections, not only
  same-parameter points.
- The result's U is the sweep direction and V is the profile; cross sections
  are constant-U curves.
- A tolerance not reached within the section/refinement budget is refused as
  SurfaceBuildError::ToleranceNotReached. No fallback.

LIMITATIONS

- Only the controlled NURBS surface is built. No seam/joint, ProjectAndTrim,
  caps, stitching, booleans or B-rep.
- The profile must be in the start normal plane.
- Deviation is sampled, not a continuous bound; a refusal at the tolerance or
  section budget is the documented outcome.
- Surface U is the sweep direction and V the profile (constant-U cross
  sections). This orientation is a property of cross_section_surf_param = U and
  must be kept when the surface is consumed.
- The Level A line-circle seam comparison needs tangent extensions; this is a
  property of the miter, not of the adapter.
