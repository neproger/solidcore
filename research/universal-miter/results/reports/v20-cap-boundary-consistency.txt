MEASUREMENTS

  line-arc:
    original start=(0,0,0)   prepared start=(0,0,0)
    original end=(0,1,0)     prepared end=(0,1,0)
    cap-plane deviations = 1.5e-17 / 8.1e-17

  arc-arc:
    original start=(-1,1,0)
    prepared start=(-1.0000000000, 1.7071067812, 0)
    original end=(-1,1,0)
    prepared end=(-1.7071067812, 1.0000000000, 0)
    cap-plane deviations = 0.7071067812 / 0.7071067812

ROOT CAUSE

  Side surfaces are built over NormalizedPlacement::path_piece. Cap planes are
  built from the original path_start/path_end. Arc-arc prepared endpoints are
  offset by 0.7071067812, so cap profile edges from the side-surface U boundary
  do not lie on the cap plane. This explains the tolerance 1.4142135624
  (2 * 0.7071) without any endpoint mismatch or periodic UV branch.

  line-arc passes because prepared and original endpoints coincide.

  the intended cap boundary, then make cap plane and cap profile edges use the
  same one. Do not adjust tolerances.
