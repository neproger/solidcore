FINDINGS

  1. The boundary solver is universal for the exact strategy: line-line and
     line-arc run the same ExactMiter code over the differential/offset
     trajectories. Only the test inputs and the Level B surface tolerance
     changed.
  2. Seam V is taken from Surface::drop_point(result_uv.y) on both side
     surfaces, not from i/K; this is required for edge/loop validation.
  3. The p6 make_face_topology TestClosureSplitRegion Unported remains after
     the split; the split completes and the exact Unported is accepted. The
     shell is validated independently.
  4. Side faces use OrientType::Opposite (inward Level B normal); caps use
     outward plane normals and Same.
  5. Line-arc curved seam and face trims validate, but volume converges only to
     about 2e-3 relative with the current Level B surface accuracy.

LIMITATIONS

  Exact miter only; hybrid (tangent/bisector limit) and adaptive-cut strategies
  are not implemented. One joint, two segments, circular profile, K=4. p6 tail
  and the 1e-9 volume integrator limit remain kernel gaps.
