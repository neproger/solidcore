VERDICT

AOIM is the correct architectural direction and the direct shared-topology
B-rep prototype is real. It is a probe for a rectangular/analytic class, not a
general or certified solution. Our Level A/B are more general and can supply
the miter loops and NURBS surfaces; the missing work is porting and validating
the AOIM topology builder on the current kernel.

SUPPORTED

- Exact line-arc miter (parabola) in the 2D suite; Level A independently has
  line_arc_is_the_analytic_parabola.
- Direct shared topology via make_shell_vertex, make_wire_edge,
  make_face_topology; no BrepIntersect, Boolean, ProjectAndTrim, StitchFaces
  in the probe.
- Shared vertices/edges; 4 edge-uses per edge (2 Same + 2 Opposite).
- 90/30/150/179 line-arc prisms; 8/18/12 and 9/21/14; both caps-first and
  sides-first orders; 2 shells/2 regions (void + solid).

NOT CERTIFIED / NOT COVERED

- Volume: brep_volume was blocked by SmSurface::DropCurve at publication; the
  probe did not measure volume (expected area * height only analytically).
- Outward orientation: orient_trimmed_surfaces was blocked by
  CalculateBoundingBox; the probe checked orientation counts, not outward
  normals.
- TestClosureSplitRegion tail: make_face_topology still returns
  Unported(topology.brep_p6) after the split (current p6.rs:2396). The simple
  prism split completes and the flags are correct; the omitted tail
  (FindAndSetInfiniteRegion, IsInnerShell, RayFire, cleanup) is not exercised.
- Shell.faces cache was empty while the faceuse list was complete.
- General rectangular profiles, holes, reflex/self-intersections, arbitrary
  profiles/NURBS, multiple path pieces/joints, and performance are not covered.
- The 68 passing tests are the 2D/adaptive/continuation suite, not the direct
  B-rep probe; the probe is manual and not in CI.
- Hybrid/tangent/adaptive strategies are research alternatives/negative
  controls; they are not the exact AOIM target and must not become fallbacks.

RELATION TO OUR WORK

Level A = exact miter source (more general than the AOIM line/arc 2D class).
Gate 0 = preprocessing.
Level B = NURBS side surface; AOIM used plane/cylinder patches for a
rectangular vertical extrusion.
v4 = not the target.
v5-v7 = useful topology experiments, not the final architecture.
v8 = production topology audit and validation reference (exact volumes:
  3.926991e-1 single/line-line, 6.168503e-1 arc, 5.890486e-1 three line-line);
  its stitch_and_orient + trim/boolean is not the AOIM assembly.
