FINDING

  The arc-arc failure is in the direct B-rep representation, not the 3D
  geometry:
  - build_direct_single splits the swept surface at K profile V boundaries and
    creates each face on a non-periodic patch;
  - production rotational sweep obtains 3D curves, UV lines and orientations
    together from Surface::create_natural_uv_trim_curves;
  - build_direct_chain instead uses one full periodic surface per piece, hand-
    built loops and independently dropped UV trims.

  Line-line and line-arc close because their seam representative is V=0.
  Arc-arc gets V~1 on the rail and V=0 on the seam edge, which an unsplit
  periodic loop cannot reconcile. Post-trim period shifts only move one side.

EVIDENCE

  - 3D geometry audit: seam-to-both-surfaces 3.779e-6 (PASS).
  - UV audit: FaceId(0)/LoopuseId(1), rail EdgeId(12) V~0.999983, seam
    EdgeId(8) V=0/0.25, check gap 0.749983.
  - align_face_periodic_uv is called; it changes EdgeId(12) to V=-1.67e-5,
    but check_brep still reports 0.749983 and adds a 1.0 gap. No regeneration
    before check.

RECOMMENDATION

  Refactor the joint/chain side-face construction to:
  1. split each side surface at its K profile V boundaries;
  2. create each K face on its own non-periodic patch with local UV;
  3. share seam vertices/edges between adjacent patches;
  4. keep the start/end caps on the outer patches;
  5. use the same path for line, line-arc and arc-arc (no special case).
