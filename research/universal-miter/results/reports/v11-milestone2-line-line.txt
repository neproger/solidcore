FINDINGS

1. The side-surface V parameter must come from dropping the seam vertices
   (result_uv.y), not from the profile contour fraction i/K. Using i/K produced
   edge/loop validation gaps; using the drop result fixed the loop endpoints.
2. The last make_face_topology still returns the p6 TestClosureSplitRegion
   Unported after the split. The split completes; the exact Unported is
   accepted and the whole shell is validated independently.
3. Side faces use OrientType::Opposite (Level B surface normal is inward);
   caps use outward plane normals and OrientType::Same.
4. The exact miter sample points have rail residual 2.3e-16; the interpolated
   seam curve deviates 8.9e-7 from both side surfaces.

LIMITATIONS

Exact line-line only; circular profile K=4; one joint/two segments. No
line-arc, arc-arc or hybrid/adaptive strategies yet. The seam between samples
is interpolated (sample points exact). p6 tail and the 1e-9 volume integrator
limit remain kernel gaps. Production sweep is used only as the volume
reference; the direct construction never calls it.
