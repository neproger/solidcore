CONSTRUCTION

build_swept_surface (Level B)
  -> split the NURBS surface at V into K=4 profile patches
  -> profile segments from each patch U-min/U-max; V rails from the full surface
  -> make_shell_vertex: 2K shared vertices
  -> make_wire_edge: K start segments, K end segments, K rails
  -> make_face_topology: K side patches + start/end caps
  -> reset_infinite_region; set_region_is_void_flags_for_nested_solids;
     refresh_cached_topology

No create_faces_with_curves, stitch_faces, project_and_trim or Boolean.
Side faces use OrientType::Opposite (the Level B surface normal is inward);
caps use outward plane normals with OrientType::Same. Start cap loop reversed,
end cap forward.

Both shells have all 6 faces and 6 faceuses; each edge has 4 edge-uses
(2 Same + 2 Opposite). Euler: V - E + F = 8 - 12 + 6 = 2 per closed shell.
The two regions are the expected void/solid pair.

VOLUME QUERY ACCURACY

brep_volume_default uses accuracy 1e-3, the same order is_outward_solid uses
for the sign. At 1e-9 the integrator returns SM_ERR (no convergence) on the
direct NURBS faces; that is a numerical integration limit, not a geometric
tolerance. The 1e-3 results match the analytic and production references to the
printed digits (1.5e-7 straight, 2.7e-6 arc). Tests assert <= 1e-5 relative.

API FINDINGS

1. Last make_face_topology still returns
   Unported("SmBrep::TestClosureSplitRegion", "topology.brep_p6") after the
   split. The split and the void/solid pair complete; the exact Unported is
   accepted for the simple prism and the shell is validated independently.
2. Side NURBS patches with OrientType::Same fail check_brep with
   "Face Loop has bad orientation" and outward=false. OrientType::Opposite
   fixes the orientation without geometry changes.
3. Shell.faces cache after refresh: 6 faces / 6 faceuses on both shells (the
   older AOIM empty-cache finding does not reproduce).
4. Volume accuracy 1e-3: documented, not silently loosened.

LIMITATIONS

N=1 only; circular profile K=4; no joints; no arbitrary/multi-curve profiles;
no open profile or closed/periodic path. p6 tail still unported for general
cases. The production sweep is used only as the volume reference; the direct
construction never calls it.
