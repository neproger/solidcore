arc-arc miter specification
===========================

contract A fixed
- seam must lie on both bounded source surfaces and inside both UV domains
- boundary_validation::validate_joint_boundary is the independent gate

arc-arc miter math
- exact sweep at fixed profile angle theta is a circle:
  rho(theta)=R +/- r*cos(theta), same arc center
- valid seam is the same-theta intersection of the two offset circles,
  restricted to both original arc domains
- generic Torus/Torus intersection is not sufficient (spurious profile-phase
  branches)
- no point in both original domains => UncoveredProfile; no tangent extension
