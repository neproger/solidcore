# Universal miter - Arc-arc geometric consistency audit
version: v17
label: arc-arc-geometric-consistency
head: 28d6f6afc (research changes uncommitted)
workspace: research/universal-miter (separate workspace; production untouched)

CASE

  arc A: centre (0,1), r=1, -180..-90
  arc B: centre (-1,0), r=1, 0..90
  circular profile r=0.25, K=4
  HybridAtJunction exact=36 junction=32, rail residual=6.995e-14

AUDIT

  surface A analytic-vs-Level-B max deviation = 4.280e-5
  surface B analytic-vs-Level-B max deviation = 4.280e-5
  seam distance to surface A                  = 3.779e-6
  seam distance to surface B                  = 3.779e-6
  seam distance to both (max)                 = 3.779e-6

CONCLUSION

  The 3D miter curve lies on both swept surfaces. The surface builder preserves
  the arc curvature/extension and the profile/normal frame to the measured
  tolerance. A wrong arc continuation, lost curvature, or a coordinate-frame
  mismatch is not supported for this case.

  The 11 check_brep failures of v16 are therefore UV trim/loop
  parameterization, not the 3D geometry. The next fix belongs in the periodic
  UV branch/loop creation, not in the surface or boundary geometry.

TEST

  arc_arc_geometric_consistency in prototype/tests/direct_joint.rs
  asserts each measured distance <= 1e-4.
